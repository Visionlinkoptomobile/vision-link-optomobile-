Who We Are

Connecting People to Better Vision

We bring professional eye care closer to the people through convenient mobile screening and outreach services.

Our Story

Vision Link OptoMobile is a team of dedicated optometrists committed to making quality eye care more accessible and convenient. Through mobile eye screenings and community outreaches, we bring professional optometric services closer to the people and help identify vision problems early.

Our Mission

To make quality eye care accessible, convenient and available to more people through professional mobile optometric services.


Our Vision

A Ghana where everyone has access to timely and quality eye care.

What We Do

Our Services

Professional screening and eye-health services tailored to the needs of individuals and organizations.

👁️

Comprehensive Eye Screening

Professional assessment of vision and basic ocular health to identify potential visual problems.

👓

Vision & Refraction Assessment

Assessment of myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism and presbyopia, with appropriate spectacle recommendations where indicated.

🏫

School Eye Screening

School-based screening for students and staff to identify undetected vision problems that may affect learning.

🏢

Corporate & Workplace Screening

Convenient workplace vision screening programs designed to support employee eye health.

⛪

Church & Community Outreaches

Eye screening and eye-health education for churches, communities, NGOs and organizations.

📚

Professional Eye-Health Education

Education on eye health, children's vision, digital eye strain, healthy visual habits and preventable visual impairment.
